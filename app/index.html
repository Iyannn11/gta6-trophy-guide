Watch out: possibly missable

No verified missable list exists yet — the game has only just come out, and PlanetGTA6 says so plainly. Two trophies carry a real warning, and both are flagged in the list below:

  • Positive Mental Attitude — GTABase reports a meditation with Lucia on a balcony in Chapter 1. The game has no mission replay, so do it the moment it is offered.
  • Actions Speak Louder — GTABase warns the Irredeemable criminal profile is permanent. Save before any big choice.

General rule until a verified list exists: keep a manual save before every big mission and every big choice. The game has 15 manual save slots (Games.gg). And keep cheats off — PowerPyx reports a cheat disables trophies, and saving stops working once one is used.

Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough is enough — there are no difficulty settings and no New Game+. GTABase puts the platinum at around 200 hours, most of it on the 100% checklist. Work the stages in order.
