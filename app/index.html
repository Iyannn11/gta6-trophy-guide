Missable trophies — the honest policy

PlayStation has not published a verified missable list for this game, so this guide does not invent one. Only two trophies are flagged “possible”, and only because they need your partner beside you, and partner availability is tied to the story: Hot Together and Retail Therapy. Do them during chapters 2–3, while partner missions are still regular. Every other trophy is repeatable or story-gated and cannot be missed.
